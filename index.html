A problem I solved

Keeping the Lock Screen score correct

In KickFlow, Lock Screen updates during a live match could arrive out of order, so fans sometimes saw an old score, and in rare cases match alerts stopped with no error.

I found the race condition with Swift Concurrency, made every update for a match run in order, and added a check that drops stale updates. Scores now stay correct through the whole match.
